**Battle of the Bros: a true test of who is the better gamer!**

## Overview
Originally inspired by the old Ludwig bro vs bro videos, Battle of the Bros is a project created to organize the game selection, score and everything there is to track in a bro vs bro challenge.

## What is it?
Battle of the Bros is a 1v1 rapid-fire minigame competition. Some popular BOTB games include: Wordle, Chess, typeracer, wikipedia speedrunning, tetris and various (mostly browser based) games. 

## How to play?
All you have to do is click this (link here eventually) link, pick your username and invite your bro! It's designed to be simple and intuitive in a way that just works.
